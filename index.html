01 / WHAT IS CAMPUSCODE?

WHERE STUDENTS BUILD.

CampusCode is an AI-powered hackathon platform — one place to run and join hackathons, instead of juggling many tools.

College hackathons run on scattered forms, WhatsApp channels, and messy spreadsheets. CampusCode brings registrations, team matching with HackMate, AI idea screening, live rounds, and verifiable certificates under a single unified dashboard.
